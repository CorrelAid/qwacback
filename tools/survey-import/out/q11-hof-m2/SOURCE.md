## Why no-instrument

The report has no questionnaire. It contains:

- p. 11: a list of the 8 general questions (wording only, no answer options);
- p. 12 (Abb. 5): a screenshot of 2 strategy questions with their options;
- Anlagen pp. 33–53: result charts, one per question, with the question as the chart title and the answer
  options as the legend.

The charts cannot be used as a verbatim source:

- Legend texts are cut off mid-word, e.g. p. 42 ("…bis auf Weiteres beibehalten und werde", "…die
  papierlose Bearb", "…so dass die Da", "…in andere Systeme übertr").
- Answer options that nobody chose are missing. On p. 52 the IT skills item has 4 options and the item
  below it has 5 ("Sehr hoch ausgeprägt"). On p. 33 the Bundesland chart lacks Berlin, Bremen and Hamburg.
- At least one legend looks copied from another chart. On p. 35 "In welchem Tätigkeitsfeld arbeiten Sie?"
  lists "(Ober-)Bürgermeister, Landrat…" as the first field, the same as in the Position chart. The tenure
  categories skip 1 year ("< 1 Jahr", "2-5 Jahre").
- The order of the items within each dimension, the scoring (1–5) and any filters are not documented.

Full wording and options are printed only for the two questions in Abb. 5. A two-item fragment
would not represent the m² instrument.
