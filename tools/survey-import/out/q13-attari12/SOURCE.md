## Transcription

- 12 items `attari_1` … `attari_12` (source numbering), one `field-list` group whose label is the instruction; `select_one attari_agree5`.
- Base language Deutsch (de) (Supplement S8 / OSF German version), English (en) from the Appendix.
- Response scale: the article states "a five-point answer format … (1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree)". English choice labels: 1 "strongly disagree", 2–4 numbers, 5 "strongly agree". **No German response labels are published**, so German choice labels are the numbers 1–5 only (not translated).
- Not in the form (scoring metadata): facet and valence. Reverse-coded (negative) items: 2, 4, 7, 8, 10, 12. Facets: cognitive 1, 4, 6, 10; affective 2, 5, 8, 11; behavioral 3, 7, 9, 12.
